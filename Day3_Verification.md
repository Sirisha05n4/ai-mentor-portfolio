## Reflection Paragraph

The claim that looked most authoritative but was actually weakest was claim #5: 'The median placement package at IITs in 2025 was ₹18.5 LPA (India Skills Report 2025).' Gemini confidently cited this figure and assigned it to a highly respected national study, making it appear entirely authentic. However, when verifying against the primary data, I discovered that the India Skills Report doesn't publish specific baseline median-salary stats for individual IIT clusters. This proved that AI models can easily bundle actual report names with entirely confabulated numbers. The lesson: confidence does not equal correctness. The verification step belongs to the human — every time.
